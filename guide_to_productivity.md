# A Simple Guide to Daily Productivity

## Introduction
Productivity isn't about doing more—it's about doing what matters most, consistently and intentionally.

## Core Principles

### 1. Start with a Clear Intention
Before your day begins, identify the 3 things that would make today successful. Focus on impact over volume.

### 2. Batch Similar Tasks
Grouping similar work reduces context-switching overhead. Block time for:
- Deep work (coding, writing, design)
- Meetings and communication
- Administrative tasks

### 3. Protect Your Peak Hours
Most people have 2-4 peak hours per day when they do their best work. Guard this time fiercely for your most important tasks.

### 4. Build in Recovery Time
Productivity requires rest. Schedule breaks, walk outside, and separate work from relaxation.

## Tools & Systems

| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Calendar blocking | Protect focus time |
| Task lists | Capture and prioritize |
| Timers (Pomodoro) | Maintain momentum |
| Note-taking | Reduce cognitive load |

## Quick Wins

✅ Start with the hardest task first  
✅ Silence notifications during deep work  
✅ Use templates to eliminate decisions  
✅ Review what worked weekly  

## Remember
Consistency beats intensity. Small daily improvements compound into remarkable results over months and years.
